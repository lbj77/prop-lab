Projections

AI % is the model’s chance he goes over (or under) this line tonight, built from his recent games, minutes, the opponent’s defense, home/away, rest and the sharp line. Hit rates are how often he went over (or under) this exact line in his last 5, 10 and 15 games, and in past games against tonight’s opponent (H2H). Streak = games in a row he hit it (green = 3 or more, red = 2 or fewer). Tap a column to sort, a name to open the player, the star to add it to your parlay.

Head to head

How to read it: each number is how players do against that defense compared with their own normal.
Over +9% they get 9% more than usual → lean over
Under −8% they get 8% less than usual → lean under
Even +2% close to normal → no real edge
¹ a key defender is out tonight, already counted in the number
